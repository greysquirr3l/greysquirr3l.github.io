shadowforge-rs

Pre-audit software

Steganography for the journalist-vs-nation-state threat model

shadowforge-rs hides encrypted payloads inside ordinary-looking cover media, then layers post-quantum cryptography, forward error correction, and operational security countermeasures on top.

Built for sources and journalists who need a payload to survive a hostile platform’s recompression pipeline — and to survive legal compulsion to decrypt.

Quick startInstallation

Capabilities

Ten steganographic techniques across image, audio, PDF, text, and corpus selection, with post-quantum protection and countermeasures for the attacks that actually matter against adversaries with nation-state budgets.

Post-quantum cryptography

ML-KEM-1024 key encapsulation and ML-DSA-87 signatures, both pure Rust NIST finalists. No liboqs, no CGo, no external crypto dependency to audit.

ml-kem · ml-dsa · argon2id · aes-256-gcm

PDF as a first-class context

Content-stream least-significant-bit embedding, metadata watermarking, and a page-render pipeline — not a bolt-on. PDFs are the format sources actually exchange.

lopdf · pdfium-render

Distribution patterns

1:1, 1:N, N:1, and N:M matrix distribution over Reed-Solomon K-of-N shards, so no single compromised carrier loses the payload.

reed-solomon-erasure · hmac-sha256

Operational security

Amnesiac mode writes nothing to disk, dual-payload deniability answers compulsion with a decoy, and dead drop mode posts to public services without a direct transfer.

amnesiac · deniable · dead drop · scrubber

Countermeasures

Adversarial embedding optimisation, camera-model fingerprint matching, and compression-survivable embedding calibrated against real platform pipelines.

adaptive · corpus · canary · timelock

Threat model first

Compelled decryption, traffic analysis, endpoint compromise, legal pressure, and stylometric identification are documented as design constraints, not footnotes.

threat model · residual risks
